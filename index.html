Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee a higher Google ranking?

No. Local visibility depends on many factors, including relevance, distance, prominence, competition and Google's systems. I focus on accurate information, quality profile maintenance and useful customer communication—not ranking guarantees.

Will you need my Google account password?

No password should be shared. Where access is needed, use Google Business Profile's authorized manager access and grant only the permissions required for the agreed work.

Do you write or buy customer reviews?

No. Reviews must come from genuine customer experiences. I can help you create a compliant process for requesting feedback and draft professional responses to reviews you receive.

What do you mean by Q&A seeding?

I help identify common questions and prepare accurate answers for customer-facing use. I do not post fabricated questions or pretend to be customers. Any Q&A activity must follow the platform's current features and policies.

Do you provide services in languages other than English?

Localization can be scoped around the languages your customers use. Language availability and review requirements should be confirmed before the project begins.
