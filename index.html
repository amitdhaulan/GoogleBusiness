No password should be shared. Authorized manager access should be used wherever access is required.

Do you provide fake reviews?

No. Reviews must come from genuine customer experiences. I can help with compliant review response processes.

What is BizTrack?

BizTrack is an Excel-based business management tool designed to help small businesses track sales, inventory and profitability. The features vary by edition.
